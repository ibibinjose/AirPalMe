### Description (Max 4,000 characters)
```text
AirPal.Me is the guest companion for hotels and residential colleges, and the operating system for the people who run them. Guests get one place for everything about their stay. Hosts get one place to run it.

YOUR STAY, IN YOUR POCKET
Check in, find your way around, and get answers without queuing at a front desk. Everything you need for your stay, from Wi-Fi details to house rules to local tips, is in one app, ready when you are.

REQUEST ANYTHING, INSTANTLY
Order housekeeping, report a maintenance issue, book a facility, or ask for a late checkout in a few taps. Track each request from submitted to done, so you never have to wonder whether anyone saw it.

DISCOVER YOUR SURROUNDINGS
Find the best nearby cafés, sights, transport, and hidden gems, recommended by your host and by fellow guests. Explore the campus or the neighbourhood like a local from your first day.

CONNECT WITH YOUR COMMUNITY
Whether you're a traveller sharing a hotel or a student living in a residential college, meet the people around you. Join house events, follow announcements, and build real friendships with your pals under the same roof.

EVENTS & EXPERIENCES
Discover dinners, tours, workshops, socials, and college events happening during your stay. Hosts can create and list events directly in the app and bring their community together.

FOR HOSTS: RUN EVERYTHING FROM ONE PLACE
Manage guest requests, send announcements, coordinate staff, publish local recommendations, and see what guests need most. One dashboard replaces scattered messages, notice boards, and spreadsheets.

YOUR STAY, REMEMBERED
Every stay adds to your history: places you've been, events you joined, and people you met. Come back and pick up right where you left off.
```
