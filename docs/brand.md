# Brand Identity: AIRPAL Ecosystem

Tokens and chrome are frozen in **`docs/AIRPAL.md` Part E**. Do not restyle off-token.

---

## 1. Brand Hierarchy

### AIRPAL (Master Brand)
* **Company / Master Brand**: AIRPAL
* **Brand Promise**: Life Made Easy.
* **Mechanism**: Turning "I want to do something" into a completed real-world experience.
* **Core Advantage**: The connection layer bridging universal identity, verified trust, smart discovery, seamless ticketing, and memorable experiences.
* **Unified Foundation**: One login, one profile, one trust engine across the ecosystem.

---

### Product 1: AirPal.Me — Personal Life & Identity
> **"Plan your day. Organise your life. Share who you are."**

* **Positioning**: "My life, organised by AirPal."
* **Audience**: Individuals, locals, travellers, and students wanting clarity, quick daily decisions, and an elegant digital identity.
* **Core Job**:
  * Organize personal daily flow without decision fatigue.
  * Curate and share digital identity via fast `@handle` link-in-bio.
  * Live context: weather, deterministic outfit advice, and local vibe.
  * Plan hangouts with friends via lightweight invite links (`/plan`).
* **Tone**: Personal, intimate, encouraging, calm, organised, effortless.

---

### Product 2: AirPal Pass — Events, Deals & Experiences
> **"Discover what's happening. Get access. Save money."**

* **Positioning**: "Your pass to experiences."
* **Audience**: Outgoers, music fans, cultural communities (Bollywood, Diwali, Dandiya, Holi), foodies, and deal seekers.
* **Core Job**:
  * Discover what's on tonight, this weekend, or next month.
  * Instant digital passbook with scannable QR tickets and Apple/Google Wallet support (`/me/tickets`).
  * Exclusive dining perks and local merchant discounts (`/deals`).
  * Frictionless door check-in operations for organisers (`/scan`).
* **Tone**: Vibrant, exciting, celebratory, accessible, trustworthy, urgent.

---

## 2. Visual System & Color Tokens

* **Master Brand Accent**: AirPal Blue `#1805db` (pressed `#0e0394`).
* **Backgrounds & Cards**: Mist `#eef1f6` (canvas), Pure White `#ffffff` (cards with `ring-1 ring-line`).
* **Typography Ink**: Primary ink `#121614`, secondary mute `#6a726c`, border `#e5e7eb`.
* **Category Accents**:
  * Amber / Gold `#f59e0b` / `#d97706` for AirPal Pass tickets, member badges, and VIP passes.
  * Emerald `#10b981` for active perks, verified badges, and completed experiences.
  * Purple `#8b5cf6` for cultural and festive celebrations.
* **Fonts**: system stack via `--font-sans` / `--font-display` in `src/index.css` (-apple-system / SF Pro on iOS, Segoe UI / Roboto elsewhere). No webfont downloads, zero external CDN dependencies.
