# App Story (Pointer)

Merged into [`AIRPAL.md`](./AIRPAL.md) (binding source of truth).

The AirPal story centers on turning intent into completed real-world experiences through two clear lenses:
- **AirPal.Me:** "My life, organised by AirPal." Your personal daily companion for daily plans, outfits, weather, calendar, and digital identity.
- **AirPal Pass:** "Your pass to experiences." Access to what's happening: live concerts, Indian cultural festivals, dining deals, and tickets.
