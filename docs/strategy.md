# Product Strategy (Pointer)

Merged into [`AIRPAL.md`](./AIRPAL.md) (binding source of truth).

The overarching strategy establishes **AIRPAL** as the master brand with two distinct product lenses:
1. **AirPal.Me — Personal Life & Identity:** ("Plan your day. Organise your life. Share who you are.")
2. **AirPal Pass — Events, Deals & Experiences:** ("Discover what's happening. Get access. Save money.")

Both lenses share the exact same user identity, database, and trust rails. See `AIRPAL.md` Parts A–C & F for full details. Full pre-rebuild content is preserved in git history.
